# Privacy Policy for Pixora
**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Pixora ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, ad-free, and account-free pixel art and sprite animation editor. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect
Pixora does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

* ❌ No account or registration required
* ❌ No analytics or tracking
* ❌ No advertising SDKs
* ❌ No crash reporting sent to external servers
* ❌ No location data
* ❌ No device identifiers
* ❌ No usage data sent anywhere

## 3. Data Stored Locally
All data created in Pixora stays on your device only:

* **Project files (`.pxs`)** — saved to your device storage
* **Exported images (PNG, GIF)** — saved to your device storage or shared via your device's share sheet

We have no access to this data.

## 4. Permissions
Pixora may request the following permissions on your device. These permissions are used solely within the app and no data is transmitted externally:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Read/Write Storage** | Save and open project files and exported images |
| **Read Media Images** | Access images for importing into the editor |

## 5. Third-Party Services
Pixora does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect data. There are no ads, no analytics platforms, and no social media integrations.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Pixora is safe for all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special risks for children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

## 7. Changes to This Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since we collect no data, material changes are unlikely.
