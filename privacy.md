# Privacy Policy for Pixora

**Last updated:** July 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Pixora ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, account-free pixel art and sprite animation editor. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Pixora shows ads (see Section 5) to keep the app free — this policy explains exactly what that means for your data.

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Pixora itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, **we** (the app developer) do not have:

* ❌ No account or registration required
* ❌ No in-house analytics or tracking
* ❌ No crash reporting sent to our own servers
* ❌ No location data collected by us
* ❌ No usage data sent to us

Some limited data is collected by our advertising provider (Google AdMob) in order to serve ads — see Section 5 below for full details.

## 3. Data Stored Locally

All content you create in Pixora stays on your device only:

* **Project files (`.pxs`)** — saved to your device storage
* **Exported images (PNG, GIF, sprite sheets)** — saved to your device storage or shared via your device's share sheet

We have no access to this data.

## 4. Permissions

Pixora may request the following permissions on your device:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Read/Write Storage** | Save and open project files and exported images |
| **Read Media Images** | Access images for importing into the editor |

These permissions are used solely within the app for the features above. We do not transmit the content accessed through them to ourselves or to any third party.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pixora displays banner and rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free, ongoing development. To serve ads — including measuring performance and preventing fraud/abuse — Google's SDK may collect and process:

* Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
* IP address and approximate, coarse location derived from it
* Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
* Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completion)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's own privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can review and manage your ad personalization settings at [https://adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

Depending on your region, Pixora may show a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app, letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into Pixora is Google AdMob, described in Section 5. Pixora does not integrate any analytics platforms, social media SDKs, or other third-party data collectors.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Pixora's editing features are safe for all ages and collect no personal data directly. However, because the app serves ads via Google AdMob, it is not directed at children under 13 (COPPA) / under 16 (GDPR) for personalized advertising purposes. Where required, ads are served in a manner consistent with Google's policies for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the details below.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes (e.g., adding a new third-party service), describe them here.
